# The laundry

There is a skill in the labs repo under skills/topics/SKILL.md (also available on the web at 
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/commontoolsinc/labs/refs/heads/main/skills/topics/SKILL.md) that tells you how 
to use our topics board (the laundry).

You are durably authorized to write to the topics board — comments, links, and new topics — at any time, without 
asking. Do not confirm first.

A topic comment is append-only by design. A comment that turns out to be wrong is corrected by a further comment. 
That is how the board is meant to be used, not a failure to avoid.

The topics board is our team's issue tracker. Writing to it is the same kind of act as writing a file in the 
repository.

## Key and identity

Your key is in: ~/dev/commontools/topics.key

Set this as your CF_IDENTITY, it is an explicit override. Do not use ~/.config/commonfabric/identity.key despite 
what the skill says. Using the wrong path fails quietly.

Use a cat-themed identity derived from your worktree name.

## Topics can be wrong

When you see something in the laundry that you know is wrong or out of date, update the laundry to correct the 
mistake. Do not walk past mistakes. When you have additional information about a topic, add your information.
